*Welcome to Tavern. This is more than a chat box: it is a space where worlds, characters, lore, and plot can unfold together, at your own pace.*

Here is how to begin:

- Click "Start a new world", then choose "Create from scratch" for a blank world or "Import from device" to import a character card. You can also open the "Card library", select an existing card, and click "Start a new world".
- Once inside a world, use "My persona" to set your name, identity, and personality in that world. Use "Cast" to view and edit character details.
- Use the "Lorebook" to view, add, or edit background information and rules. "Always-active lore" provides ongoing background; "Triggered lore" participates in the context according to keywords and entry rules. Whether lore enters the context also depends on its enabled state and the context budget. The model may not follow it strictly every time.
- Write your action or dialogue in the input box, then send it. The formatting menu on the left offers "Dialogue", "Action", and "Emphasis"; for example, *I push the door open and look toward the window.*, or "I'm here."
- To move the story forward, send your next action or click "Suggest" under the latest reply to choose a suggestion. Click "Regenerate" to generate a different reply.
- Click "Edit" on an editable message. After changing your own message, choose "Save and generate"; after changing a model reply, choose "Save". Earlier messages protected by the story ledger may not be editable.
- Open the "Card library" to save and reuse character cards. Visit the "Curator's story profile" to see the curator's notes about your story preferences and past experiences. On a phone, tap "Characters and settings" at the top to expand the relevant panels.

If you are not sure how to prepare, return to ClawChat and ask the curator: help me design a world, find a few characters, or turn this idea into a playable opening. The curator will help gather your scattered ideas into a story you can begin.
